WHAT THIS IS

The texts of 178 official acts that bear on the museums and cultural heritage of occupied
Crimea, Sevastopol and the Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions, or on the
Russian museum system those museums were brought into. The acts were signed between
2014-03-17 and 2026-10-23. They are in Russian. Each file is the act's own text. Two of
the files are annexes of one act.


SELECTION

An official act here is a dated document issued by a named public body: a law, a treaty of
accession, a decree, a resolution or order of a government, an order or letter of a ministry
or agency, a state programme, or the regulations of a body. Acts of the occupation
authorities are included and are filed under the names those authorities use for themselves.
That describes the source. It does not recognize it.

Not included: acts signed before 2014, drafts, and documents that are not acts (guidance,
manuals, standards, forms).


SOURCES

The acts were gathered from five places.

1. The acts cited in two studies written for CURIA Lab on how the museums and heritage bodies
   of Crimea and of Donetsk region were brought into the Russian system.
2. The federal acts that govern the Museum Fund and the State Catalogue of the Museum Fund, the
   acts that admitted the occupied territories to the Russian Federation, and the acts these
   name as governing entry into the Catalogue or exclusion from it.
3. Searches of official sites for the acts that set up culture ministries and heritage bodies
   in Sevastopol and in the Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions, for the Ministry of
   Culture's letters on the relaunch of the State Catalogue in 2015, and for the Donetsk law on
   museums of 2024.
4. The acts the Ministry of Culture posts for museums on the documents page of the State
   Catalogue's site, goskatalog.ru, as downloaded on 18 June 2025.
5. Acts of the Donetsk authorities collected from their acts portal in November 2025.

Each text comes from the official publication where it could be reached, and otherwise from a
legal database or from the copy posted on the State Catalogue's site. The index gives the
source of each file.


COMPLETENESS

This is as full a list as we were able to gather. It has not been confirmed to be complete.
An act that none of the five sources names is not here, nor is an unpublished act, nor an act
whose text could not be obtained. Coverage is thinner for the Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and
Kherson regions than for the federal acts, Crimea and Donetsk region.


THE FILES

- Each file is named by the date of signing, the authority and the act's number.
- 157 files are the PDF as officially published or posted. Many are scans without a text layer.
- 23 files are renderings of a saved web page, a text file or a Word file. A box on the first
  page names the act and the source and says the file is a rendering.
- 19 files have no web address in the index. They are copies posted on the State Catalogue's
  site.
- Some texts are consolidated editions and not the text as signed. The file or its title in
  the index says so.
- Titles in English are ours.
